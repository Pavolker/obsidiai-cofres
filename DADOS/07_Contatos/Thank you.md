You're now running the full version of Scrivener and are ready to begin writing.

The best way of getting of getting to know Scrivener is to go through the interactive tutorial available from its Help menu. There are other sections in the tutorial that you can return to later to learn more.

The main thing to remember is that, although Scrivener contains a wealth of features, you should just use what you find most useful and ignore everything else until you need it. Just learn how to get around in the binder, split the editor, and use the different view modes to begin with. That's really all you need to know to take advantage of Scrivener's main benefits for long writing projects.
